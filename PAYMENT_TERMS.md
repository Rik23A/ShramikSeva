## 4. Subscription Activation & Validity

### 4.1 Activation
- Subscriptions are activated immediately after successful payment.
- The employer will receive an in-app notification and email confirmation.
- A downloadable invoice (PDF) will be generated automatically.

### 4.2 Validity Period
- Each subscription is valid for its stated duration from the date of activation.
- Validity is calculated in **calendar days** (not business days).
- Example: A 30-day plan purchased on 1st March expires on 31st March at 11:59 PM IST.

### 4.3 Expiry & Grace Period
- Employers will receive reminder notifications at **7 days** and **1 day** before expiry.
- After expiry, job posts will be **paused** (not deleted) and database access will be restricted.
- Employers have a **3-day grace period** to renew at the same rate before their data access is fully restricted.
- Renewal after the grace period will start a fresh subscription cycle.

## 5. Usage Policy

### 5.1 Database Unlocks
- Each database unlock reveals one worker's contact information.
- Unlocks are **non-transferable** and expire with the subscription period.
- **Unused unlocks do not carry over** to the next subscription.

### 5.2 Active Job Posts
- The number of active (live) job posts at any given time is limited by the plan.
- Completed or closed jobs **do not** count toward the active job limit.
- If the limit is reached, the employer must close an existing post before creating a new one.

### 5.3 Location Changes
- Employers can change the location of their posted jobs up to the plan-specified limit.
- Location change count resets with each new subscription.

### 5.4 Worklog Access
- Workers' daily work verification data (OTP verification, start/end photos, GPS data, attendance hours) is accessible only with **Premium plan** or the **Worklog Access Add-on**.
- Worklog data is retained for the duration of the job + 90 days.

- Example: If an employer upgrades from Basic (₹2,350) to Pro (₹4,999) after using 15 of 30 days, a credit of ₹1,175 is applied, and ₹3,824 is charged.
